There was some discussion of using ChatGPT's *Deep Research* mode for
literature searches the other day, which isn't something I'd tried before. I
recently finished the first "mini-project" I had been planning as part of this
class, and I wanted to find the direction to go in next. So I prompted ChatGPT
with:

> I am interested in finding papers that use dynamical systems methods
> (continuation, bifurcation analysis, etc.) to examine low-dimensional models
> of cloud processes, in particular cirrus and contrails. I would like to see
> some examples of where these approaches can provide interesting physical
> insights into simplified models.

and I got [this report](/ian/files/low-dim-dyn-sys-clouds.pdf). It's really
pretty good, and did a good job of finding the latest interesting papers and
extending the search parameters a little to find relevant things in adjacent
fields. It even made some little diagrams to explain what was going on.
